# Code of Conduct

We want SAYA CLI to be a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Participants
must be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and keep
private data and credentials out of discussions and bug reports.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate
exposure of secrets or customer data are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers of the `databook-studio`
organization rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
